Cookies and storage

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Draft: the parts marked [FILL IN] are not filled in yet, so this page is not final.

In short

  • LIFE/OS sets no cookies, and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It does store things in your browser, on your device: what you write in the app (that is where it is saved) and an offline copy of the app's own files.
  • We consider each of these strictly necessary for the app you chose to use. Storage like that needs no consent under the law, so LIFE/OS shows no consent banner.
  • Nothing here is ever sent to us. You can remove all of it at any time.

The rule we follow

In Norway, storing or reading information on a user's device (cookies, local storage and similar technologies) needs the user's informed consent, unless it is strictly necessary to deliver a service the user has explicitly asked for (ekomloven, the Electronic Communications Act of 2024, § 3-15; the same rule as article 5(3) of the EU ePrivacy Directive). In our assessment, everything LIFE/OS stores falls under that exception: it is there only so the app you opened can save your work and open again. The table below gives our reason for each entry.

Cookies

LIFE/OS sets no cookies at all, and the app loads nothing from other websites that could set them: its typefaces, pictures and code come from its own website (see the privacy policy).

What the app stores in your browser

These entries are kept in your browser's storage for the website's address (local storage, unless the table says otherwise). They stay on your device: the app never sends them to us. Only with Sync on are some of them sent, by you, to your own GitHub repository (see the privacy policy). Sync only works with a private repository: it checks, and sends nothing to one that isn't private. Each version there is named after the part of the app and the kind of device (for example "Notes · Mac").

Name What it holds Why it is needed How long
lifeos:v1:tasks, notes, habits, calendar, pomodoro, training, finance, recovery, health Everything you enter: tasks, notes, habits and ticks, events (and the reminders you set on tasks and events), focus sessions, workouts, tracked subscriptions and daily results, your recovery trackers (what you stay away from and the days you relapsed, which can be health data), and on the Health page your weights, your goal weight and kg or lb (health data) Saving your work is the service itself: LIFE/OS has no server of its own, so your browser is where it is kept (and, only if you turn Sync on, your own GitHub repository). Until you delete it
lifeos:v1:settings, home, calendar-prefs, habits-insights, habits-ticker Your choices: template, light or dark, date format and units, start page, the Home layout, which views and panels are open, and your reminder settings (on or off, the sound, the time for all-day items, the default reminders) Remembers settings you chose yourself, so the app looks and opens the way you set it. Until you delete it
lifeos-files (an IndexedDB database) Pictures you added to a task's page, made smaller (at most 1600 pixels on the longest side) Shows them in the task. Pictures are kept apart from the rest because local storage is too small for them. Sync never sends them. Until you use Delete all my data or clear the site's data. Taking a picture out of a page doesn't free its space yet.
lifeos:sync:config, lifeos:sync:state Only once you turn Sync on: your sync key, your GitHub user name and the repository's name; a copy of your data as it was last synced, used to merge changes Sync, which you turned on, can't work without them. The key never leaves the device except to sign requests to GitHub. Until you click Turn off sync. A sync running at that moment stops and doesn't save them again.
lifeos:backup:before-sync:…, lifeos:backup:before-restore:… A safety copy of your data, made before Sync replaces this device's data or before you restore a backup file Protects your own data from being lost by an action you started. The newest three are kept; older ones are removed by themselves
lifeos:pomodoro-alert:chime, lifeos:pomodoro-alert:notice Which focus session's end was already announced With several windows open, only one rings the bell or shows the notice. Replaced by the next session
lifeos:reminders-fired, lifeos:reminders-snoozed Which reminders this device has already shown (and since when it keeps reminders); the reminders you snoozed, and until when Each reminder you set comes once: with several windows open, only one shows it, and one that came while the app was closed is listed once when it opens. A snooze brings it back when you asked for it. Forgotten after 30 days; a snooze once it has come. Never synced.
lifeos:clipboard (session storage) Items you copied with the app's own right-click Copy Makes the app's Paste work. Until the tab or window closes
Offline copy (cache storage, names starting with lifeos-) The app's own files: its pages, styles, code, typefaces, pictures and icons. None of your data. Lets the app open and work without an internet connection, as an installed app does, and load quickly. Replaced with each new version; removed by Delete all my data or by clearing the site's data

Two more entries exist only on test copies that developers run on their own computer (lifeos:treat-as-real and lifeos:sync-api, in session storage); the LIFE/OS website never sets them.

Your browser itself also remembers some answers you give it for the site, such as whether notifications are allowed and whether its storage may be kept when the disk runs low. Those are the browser's records, not the app's.

The website's host

The website is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). LIFE/OS asks GitHub to set nothing in your browser, and the pages contain no GitHub code. GitHub's servers do see your IP address when they deliver the files; the privacy policy explains that.

How to remove it

In LIFE/OS, Settings → Sync & backup → Delete all my data removes every entry above, and the offline copy, from that browser. You can also clear the website's data in your browser, or uninstall the installed app with Also delete data from Chrome ticked: the steps are in the privacy policy. Either way your data on that device is gone for good, so download a backup first if you want to keep it.

If this ever changes

We will not add analytics, advertising, social media buttons, embedded videos, third-party typefaces or anything else that stores or reads information on your device for a purpose that isn't strictly necessary, unless we first ask for your consent. Such a request would explain each purpose and who is behind it, offer Reject as plainly as Accept, have nothing ticked in advance, keep the app usable if you say no, and let you change your mind later as easily. This page and the privacy policy would be updated before any such change.
