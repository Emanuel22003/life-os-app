2. What LIFE/OS is

LIFE/OS is a personal organiser in your browser: tasks, notes, habits, a calendar, a focus timer, a training log and a list of your subscriptions, with a Home overview. It runs on your device and saves what you enter there. It has no account and no server of its own. You can optionally sync it between your computers through your own GitHub account (section 7).

LIFE/OS helps you organise yourself. It is not medical, financial or training advice: the numbers it shows (streaks, the LIFE index, estimated maximums, subscription totals) are calculations from what you entered, and only as accurate as that.

6. Your data and backups

Your data lives in your browser on your device. That means:

  • it can be lost if the device breaks or is lost, if you or your browser clear the site's data, if you use a private window, or if you uninstall the app and choose to delete its data;
  • we can't see it, and so we can't restore it for you;
  • keeping copies is up to you: use Settings → Download a backup regularly, and keep the files somewhere safe. Sync also keeps a copy and its history in your GitHub repository.

7. Sync and your GitHub account

Sync is optional. It works through your own GitHub account and a private repository in it (Sync only works with a private one), under the agreement you have with GitHub (GitHub's Terms of Service). GitHub, not us, provides that storage. You are responsible for your GitHub account and for keeping your sync key secret.

Sync merges changes from your computers. When the same thing was changed in two places, the more recent change wins. When you connect a computer that already has data while GitHub has data too, LIFE/OS asks you which to keep. Download a backup before connecting such a computer.
