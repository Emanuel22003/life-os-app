Emails

LIFE/OS sends no emails today: it has no account and doesn't know your email address. If it ever does send emails:

  • marketing emails (news, offers) will be sent only to people who have agreed to receive them in advance (markedsføringsloven, the Marketing Control Act, § 15);
  • every marketing email will carry a one-click unsubscribe link, free of charge, and unsubscribing will take effect at once;
  • emails needed for a purchase, such as a receipt, will be about that purchase only.
