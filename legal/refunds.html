In short

  • Today LIFE/OS is free. Nothing is charged, and we never ask for payment details. This page is here so the rules are clear before anything costs money.
  • If a paid plan comes: you can change your mind within 14 days and get your money back. If you asked for the paid features to start at once, you pay only for the part you used, or, where the law allows it for digital content and you agreed to it at checkout, that right ends when delivery starts (section 4).
  • You can cancel a subscription at any time; it then stops at the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid, within 14 days, free of charge.

1. Today: nothing is charged

LIFE/OS has no paid plan. It has no checkout, no subscription and no in-app purchases, and it never asks for card or payment details. So there is nothing to refund or cancel today. Everything below applies only once a paid plan exists, and we will update this page before that happens.

3. Your right of withdrawal (14 days)

When you buy a paid plan online you have the right to withdraw from the purchase (angrerett) within 14 days of the day you bought it, without giving a reason (angrerettloven, the Norwegian Cancellation Act, §§ 20 and 21).

To withdraw, tell us clearly before the 14 days are over: write to [FILL IN: contact email], or use the withdrawal form below. It is enough that you send your message before the deadline.

4. When paid features start straight away

Paid features in an app usually start the moment you buy them. If you want that within the 14 days, the law has us ask you to request it expressly and to acknowledge what it means for your right of withdrawal (angrerettloven §§ 19 and 22, letters c and n). Our checkout will ask for this with a box you tick yourself; it is never ticked in advance. If you don't tick it, you keep your full 14 days. We then confirm the purchase, your request and your acknowledgement to you in writing, for example by email (§ 18).

What an early start means depends on whether the paid plan counts as a service or as digital content; the checkout will say which:

  • A service (such as a subscription to features that keep running): you can still withdraw within the 14 days. You then pay only for the part you received up to then, in proportion to the full price (angrerettloven § 26). The right ends only once the service has been delivered in full (§ 22 c).
  • Digital content (something delivered once, such as a download): the right of withdrawal ends once delivery has started, but only if you expressly agreed to the early start, acknowledged that you lose the right, and we confirmed this to you in writing (§ 22 n).

5. Refunds

  • We refund everything you paid, minus only a proportional amount where section 4 says so, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal (angrerettloven § 24).
  • The money goes back the same way you paid, unless you expressly agree to something else.
  • A refund never costs you a fee.

6. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a subscription at any time, as easily as you started it: online, the same way you subscribed, or by writing to [FILL IN: contact email]. Cancelling stops the next payment. You keep the paid features until the end of the period you already paid for, and we never charge you again after you cancel.

After the withdrawal period, a period that has already started is not refunded when you cancel, unless something was wrong (section 7). Cancelling never touches your data: it stays on your device.

7. If something is wrong

If a paid feature doesn't work as promised, tell us as soon as you can. You then have the rights that the Norwegian act on digital content and services for consumers (digitalytelsesloven) gives you: we must put it right, and if we can't do that within a reasonable time, you can get a price reduction or end the agreement and get back what you paid for the time it didn't work. These rights come in addition to the right of withdrawal.
